Quick Guide
1. Read Budget Health
Excellent means no action is waiting. Good means you are safe but still have a task. Caution deserves attention soon. Action required should be handled first.
2. Follow Today’s Priority
Ledger places the highest-impact item first. Resolve red before yellow, then finish ordinary funding work.
3. Bring Ready to Assign to $0
A positive amount is not extra spending money until you intentionally assign it. A negative amount means the plan exceeds the money available.
4. Log spending promptly
Ledger can only be trustworthy when purchases, payments, and income are recorded.
5. Check the envelope before buying
Use Can I afford this? for a quick answer. The envelope balance—not the checking balance—controls the decision.
